Nine out of every ten rupees pay for the person's time. So the cheapest way to make ads cheaper is to save that person time.

2. Faster means cheaper

A studio pays the same every month, however many ads it makes. So the more ads one person makes in a month, the less each ad costs.

When efficiency goes up, each ad costs less

Think of the month's cost as one pie. Each slice is one ad. UAgenius makes the person more efficient, so the same pie is cut into more slices, and every slice gets smaller.

Today: x ads a monthOne ad = C ÷ x
→
With UAgenius: y ads a monthOne ad = C ÷ y, a smaller slice, because y is more than x

Same pie, more slices. The month costs the studio about the same. Because the person makes more ads with UAgenius, each ad costs less.

The pies are drawn with x = 10 and y = 14 as an example. The orange slice in each is one ad.

6. Our pricing model, and why

Credits only, for now: 1 credit = 1 second of finished ad. Studios buy a pack of credits, use them, and top up when they run out. No seat fees.

Every competitor above sells credits. None of them charges just per seat or per ad. This is why credits work for us too:

Each ad costs a different amountOne ad can cost us ₹105 and another ₹315, depending on how many tries it needs. Credits let the price follow the cost. One guide says 5% of users can drive 80% of the bill source.
Top up whenever you needA studio buys only what it needs, uses it and recharges. Layer sells one-time packs source, InVideo sells extra credits from $5 source, and Higgsfield sells packs that last 90 days source.
We change credits, not pricesWhen an AI model's price goes up or down, we change how many credits an action uses. The price of a credit stays the same, so we don't have to keep re-pricing.
One simple credit, no seat fees"No one wants to manage 20 different credit systems" secondhand. Seconds of finished ad are easy to understand, and the whole ad team can use one account.
