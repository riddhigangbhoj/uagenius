How I used AI on this project

UAgenius · Riddhi Gangbhoj · Nov 17, 2026 · Home

I used AI as a research and writing helper. It collected data, checked sources, did the maths and turned my notes into pages. The questions, the trade-offs and every final call were mine.

What I did

  • Interviewed Hritik at Kinetic Age, an in-house ad maker
  • Built the Target Audience doc and scored AppBroda's 98 game studios in Table 1
  • Tested competitors hands-on and recorded the tests
  • Designed and built the prototype
  • Made every call in the log below

What AI helped with

  • Claude (Claude Code): research, competitor teardowns, first drafts of pages, charts, cost maths, publishing to GitHub Pages
  • GPT (Codex, cheap model): collecting facts and quotes with source links, for market size, retention and competitor pricing
  • Web search and Reddit for user complaints and market data

How I kept facts honest

Every number in the build memo is labelled verified (read on the source page), secondhand (quoted from another source) or estimate (our own assumption), with a link next to it. I asked AI to list every weak number before I finalised the docs.
