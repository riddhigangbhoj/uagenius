Our intent

UAgenius cuts out the repetitive work of making ads with AI tools. It learns your brand and your game once, so you can turn an idea into a finished video fast, with the same logo, font, colours, character and voice every time.

18-month moat

Our moat is retention.

Each advantage below is something we do so that studios keep using UAgenius, and each one gets stronger the longer they stay.

AdvantageWhat we'll do, and what it leads toWhen, and why then
DistributionWe'll offer UAgenius first to studios already working with AppBroda, so they try it without a long sales process. They've trusted AppBroda for 1.7 years on average.Day 1 to month 6. They can be reached on launch day, before we have any other way to sell.
Brand memoryWe'll save each studio's logo, fonts, colours, voice and characters once, so every ad comes out on-brand and leaving means typing it all in again.From the first ad, stronger every month. Memory grows with each ad, so by month 6 there's a lot to lose.
Fast iterationWe'll fix what studios complain about every week, so they don't quit. AI tools lose users fast source.Day 1 to month 18. Most users leave in the first weeks if things go wrong.
Workflow lock-inWe'll keep briefs, approvals, team members and past ads in UAgenius, so their weekly routine runs through us and switching means rebuilding it.Month 3 to 12. It takes a few months to build up an ad library and a routine.
Performance data loopWe'll combine AppBroda's ad data with each studio's results, so their ads get better the longer they stay.Month 12 to 18. Needs enough ads per genre first.
Ad platform integrationsWe'll send ads straight to ad networks, starting with AppBroda's, and bring results back, so switching tools breaks their pipeline.Month 12 to 18. Built once the core is stable. The data loop depends on it.
