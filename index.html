UAgenius

PM assignment · Riddhi Gangbhoj · Nov 17, 2026

An AI agent that turns a brief into a finished, on-brand video ad for mobile game studios. It remembers the studio's brand once, and it reaches studios through AppBroda's distribution.

UAgenius seems worth experimenting with, because AppBroda gives us distribution. If we can't get retention, we should close the experiment.
