Fruit Ninja Classic

The brief I gave

"Need a punchy 15s TikTok ad for Fruit Ninja Classic, adults who remember the original. Real slicing gameplay, not fake game scenes. Bright orange and navy. English voice, upbeat instrumental. 9:16 draft. Official product halfbrick.com/games/fruit-ninja and CTA on the App Store. No price or offer claims. Use the attached official logo, gameplay and Libre Franklin font. Keep it simple. Use real gameplay for the opener as well. No generated scenes."

Attached: the official logo, gameplay footage and the font. One follow-up: "Keep the full gameplay frame visible, and keep the captions clear of the score."

The script the agent wrote

"Remember this feeling? Slice fruit. Chase that next combo. Fruit Ninja Classic. Play now."
