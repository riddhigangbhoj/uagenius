I gave two tools the brief's sample idea: a gym protein-bar ad with a brand logo and an "Order on Blinkit" call to action. Then I asked each for one change, the way a studio would.

Because of budget, I tested two tools hands-on: Layer and Creatify. The other three (Higgsfield, InVideo, Scenario) are covered from their public docs in the Competitor Teardown. I also know these problems first-hand: at Kinetic Age I made 15 AI video ads from 201 raw AI clips. Some of them, good and bad, are on the Why me page.

Layer

Try 1: "Use the same guy, add this logo, and make him exercise, struggling." The logo is on his tank top.
Try 2: "Now add that logo in the last clip with the Order on Blinkit line." The logo moved to the end, and it was gone from his tank top.

What worked

  • Fast. The videos were ready quickly.
  • I could pick any scene it made and use it as the reference for my next prompt. It works a bit like memory for the project.

What broke

  • It couldn't keep the logo on the T-shirt. When I asked for the logo at the end, it dropped it from the shirt.

Creatify

"I want this man to be a woman now." The person changed, but the voice did not.

What broke

  • The woman still speaks in the man's voice. Only the picture changed.
  • A Creatify watermark covers the whole video. Removing it needs a paid subscription.

What this shows

Problem I hitHow UAgenius handles it

The voice didn't follow the change (Creatify)Voice is part of the saved brand, and the reviewer checks voice and picture together
Picking a scene as reference helped (Layer)We go further: the brand, characters and voice are remembered across every ad, not just one project
