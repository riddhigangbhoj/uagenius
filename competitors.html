What it wants to be

Replace the studio's motion designer: a brief goes in, a finished on-brand game ad comes out.

How the agent is set up

One main agent plans the ad, and a separate editor agent cuts it together.

Creatify

What it wants to be

Own the whole ad loop: make the ad, launch it, read the results and make the next one.

How the agent is set up

One main agent hands the work to sub-agents, like a small creative team. A second agent runs the ads after launch.

Who does what
Main agentCreatify Agent
Sub-agents
ResearcherScriptwriterCasting directorProducerEditor
AI modelsAurora, Boreal, Seedance, Kling
Separate agentAI Media Buyer: runs the ads and reads the results
What it remembers

Your logo, colours and product facts, and the same characters across ads.

Sources: Creatify Agent Ads for games AI Media Buyer Pricing page
The agent plans
You paste a store link or gameplay and pick the type of ad.
You choose the game and the ad typeSo it knows what to make
Creatify's agent splits the job between sub-agents: one researches, one writes the script, one picks the actors, one plans the shoot.
It shows you the plan, the script and the characters.
You approve the planSo it doesn't make video you'll reject
AI models it uses: Aurora (its own, for actors), Seedance and Kling for video
The AI models make it
The AI models cut your real gameplay and add the hook, a voice and an actor.

The ad is ready to launch on Meta, TikTok or Google.
You approve the launchNothing goes live without you
A second agent watches the results and learns which hooks win.

Let anyone make a full video just by typing. Built for any business, not for games.

An AI art engine trained on your game's style. It makes the parts, not the finished ad.

How the agent is set up

One assistant that runs the tools for you when you ask.

Who does what
Main agentGenie assistant
Sub-agents
None mentioned
Genie calls 90+ tools directly
AI modelsVeo, Kling, Seedance, Flux
What it remembers

Your trained art style, plus notes at company, project and personal level.

You give it your game artSo it learns your style
